Discover the Wonderful World of Oceans

Oceans are home to millions of living organisms. We must protect marine life from pollution, climate change and other human activities.

Ocean Ecosystems

Open Ocean

The open ocean is home to whales, dolphins, fish and many other marine animals.

Coral Reefs

Coral reefs provide food and shelter for a large number of marine species.

Mangrove Forests

Mangroves protect coastlines and provide shelter for young fish and other animals.

Meet Our Marine Animals

Sea Turtle

Sea turtles help maintain healthy marine ecosystems. They face threats from plastic pollution, fishing nets and habitat loss.

Sharks

Sharks play an important role in ocean food chains. Many species are threatened by overfishing and the loss of their habitats.

Blue Whale

The blue whale is the largest animal on Earth. It feeds mainly on tiny shrimp-like animals called krill.

Threats to Our Oceans

  • Plastic pollution and waste
  • Climate change and rising ocean temperatures
  • Overfishing
  • Damage to coral reefs and marine habitats
